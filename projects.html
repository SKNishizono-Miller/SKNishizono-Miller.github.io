Projects

Aeolus 2 Design Project

Sep 2023 – Dec 2023
Final year group design project. Designed the platform of the next-generation Aeolus wind profiling weather satellite. I worked as the mssion analyst using STK to design the orbit, determine eclipse time and delta-V for orbital maintenance. Assisted on the structural analysis (FEA) to determine strucutral performance at launch. Used Agile to meet deadlines. Presented PDR and FDR to industry professionals.

UKSEDS Olympus Rover Trials

Developed a Mars Rover for the Mars Sample Collect mission as part of an inter-university competition. I worked on the communications subsystem, worked as the lead software engineer, assisted in the manufacture of the rover and was the operator on the competition day.

UAV Wing Design

Collaborated with 31 students to design a UAV wing for a 2.5m span fixed-wing UAV. I worked as the primary CAD engineer using Fusion 360. Experiemental modal analysis was performed to determine the modes of the wing. The wing was then destructively tested to find critical load to verify design.
